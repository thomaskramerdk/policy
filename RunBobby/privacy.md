# Privacy Policy for Run, Bobby, Run

**Last Updated: July 19, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Run, Bobby, Run ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile game **Run, Bobby, Run** (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please do not access the App.

This disclosure is structured to align with [Apple's App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) and the data categories used in App Store Connect.

## 2. Information We Collect

Run, Bobby, Run is a single-player endless-runner game designed with privacy in mind. **We do not operate a backend server, and the App does not contain any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.** The only data involved comes from Apple's own frameworks, described below.

### 2.1 Game Center (Apple)

The App uses **Apple GameKit** to let you submit your best scores to per-theme and all-time **leaderboards** and view them.

- **What is collected:** Your Game Center player identity and the scores you achieve are sent directly to **Apple's Game Center service**, not to us. We never see or store your Game Center identity, friends list, or scores on any server we control.
- **Purpose:** App Functionality (leaderboards).
- **Linked to User:** Handled entirely by Apple under your Game Center account.
- **Used for Tracking:** No.
- **Third-party collection:** Apple (Game Center). See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Game Center is optional — if you are not signed in, or decline authentication, the App continues to work normally and simply skips leaderboard submission.

### 2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following is stored only in **UserDefaults** on your device and is never transmitted anywhere by us:

- **Game progress and stats:** best scores, coins, unlocked skins/themes, and mission/achievement progress.
- **Preferences:** sound and haptics settings, and other in-app settings.

This data stays on your device, is included in your device's normal iOS backups (iCloud/iTunes, per your iOS settings), and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

### 2.3 Sharing a Score Card

The App lets you optionally generate and share a score card image (e.g., your run result) using Apple's native **Share Sheet** (`ShareLink`/`UIActivityViewController`). This only happens if you actively choose to share, and the image is handed off to whichever app or service you pick (Messages, Instagram, etc.) — we do not receive a copy and are not involved in that transfer.

### 2.4 Data We Do Not Collect

- Name, email, phone, or postal address.
- Precise or coarse location.
- Health or fitness data.
- Contacts, photos, or other media libraries (beyond the score card you choose to export).
- Advertising or tracking identifiers (IDFA), and we do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not track you.
- Payment information — the App has no in-app purchases at this time.

## 3. How We Collect Information

We do not collect information directly. All data described in §2.1 is exchanged between your device and Apple's Game Center service, under Apple's own authentication and privacy controls. Everything else (§2.2) stays on your device.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not receive any of your data, we do not use, sell, rent, or share it. Any use of Game Center data (§2.1) is governed by Apple, not by us.

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

### 5.1 Apple (Game Center)

Leaderboard identity and scores, handled entirely by Apple.
Privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not integrate any other third-party SDK (no analytics, no advertising network, no crash reporter, no backend).

## 6. Data Storage and Security

All game data lives locally on your device via Apple's standard `UserDefaults` storage, protected by iOS's own security model. We do not operate any servers that store your data.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data is removed automatically when you uninstall the App, or you can reset your progress via the in-app settings if available. To remove your Game Center data, use Apple's Game Center and Screen Time controls, or contact Apple directly.

## 8. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and "Tracking"

The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not display the ATT prompt, because it contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect or store any personal data ourselves, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. For Game Center data, use Apple's account tools. If you have any privacy questions, contact us at §12.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the App contains no accounts, ads, or analytics, no personal data is collected regardless of age.
